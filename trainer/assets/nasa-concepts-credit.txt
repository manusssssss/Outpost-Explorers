NASA Langley Research Center concept assets used in this independent educational scene:

Space Exploration Vehicle 3D Resource: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/space-exploration-vehicle/
Habitat Demonstration Unit 3D Resource (parts 1 and 2): https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/habitat-demonstration-unit/

The Space Exploration Vehicle is a research concept, not a vehicle flown on a mission. The Habitat Demonstration Unit is a terrestrial habitat prototype/demonstrator, not an operational lunar or Mars base. Model materials were recoloured in this project to remove visible institutional markings. NASA did not create, approve, or endorse this project.

The Space Exploration Vehicle uses Draco mesh compression. Three.js loads the decoder from Google’s public gstatic CDN (version 1.5.7); the decoder is not bundled with this project.
